Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-10-19
  Features:
    - Teach serf: hold a blueprint or a whole blueprint book and click Teach serf in the toolbar (or ctrl+shift+T, or /serf learn [name]). Every blueprint goes into your serf's design library, named from its label.
    - Blueprints held straight from Game blueprints work everywhere; My blueprints work in single player (on a server, drag it to Game blueprints or your inventory first).
    - /serf join works as another name for /serf add.
  Changes:
    - Placing clears trees and rocks in the way (with mining time), rotates or upgrades what's already there like a click would, and lines coordinates up with the grid.
    - Building ghosts clears trees and rocks in their way.
    - Pole lines hop by wire reach over belts, machines and water.
    - Belt and pipe lines go under crossings and water with undergrounds.
    - Belt lines built in legs chain up: a line from the last belt of another continues it around the corner, a line to an existing belt feeds into it (never head-on), and missing undergrounds or belts get hand-crafted.
    - Walking to a spot nobody can stand on ends at the nearest free spot; a serf caught on a corner wiggles free.
    - diagnose understands fluids (petroleum gas, steam, water, crude oil).
    - Clearer messages when take, insert, mine, place or find can't do what was asked.
