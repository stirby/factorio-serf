Version: 0.3.4
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Serf tasks window (Ctrl+Shift+J, the Tasks button on the serf panel, or /serf tasks): what each serf is doing, every step of its plan (done, now, failed, to do), its queue and what it finished lately.
    - Serfs read your map tags (your names for places and purpose-built chests) and can put their own labelled tags on the map.
    - Serfs can mark an area's trees and rocks (only those) for your construction bots to clear.
  Changes:
    - Serfs show on the map: a dot in their color with their name, the same size at any zoom.
    - A serf puts items in a storage chest set to one item only if it's that item, as robots do.
    - On other planets a serf reads its own planet: storage, base checks, ghosts, alerts and production no longer default to Nauvis.
    - The base check gives an example spot for each problem (starved, blocked, no power) instead of one for the whole group.

Version: 0.3.3
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - A serf can hop to another planet's spawn (any planet someone has been to), but only empty-handed: it stores everything it carries in chests first. Armor, guns and ammo go along.
  Changes:
    - A serf app taking over a serf from a save (one another player's app had) now gets it, instead of the panel saying no serf is connected.
    - A test copy of a world (biters removed by the serf's test tools) warns everyone who joins not to save it over their real game.

Version: 0.3.2
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - /serf leave [name] sends a serf out of the game and /serf join [name] brings it back next to you, with all its items (armor keeps its equipment, items their quality). "@serf leave" and "@serf join" in chat do the same.
  Changes:
    - The serf panel, the state over a serf's head and the Teach serf shortcut show only while its app is connected and it's in the game.
    - /serf join brings back a serf that left; with none away it calls one as /serf add does.

Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-10-19
  Features:
    - Teach serf: hold a blueprint or a whole blueprint book and click Teach serf in the toolbar (or ctrl+shift+T, or /serf learn [name]). Every blueprint goes into your serf's design library, named from its label.
    - Blueprints held straight from Game blueprints work everywhere; My blueprints work in single player (on a server, drag it to Game blueprints or your inventory first).
    - /serf join works as another name for /serf add.
  Changes:
    - Placing clears trees and rocks in the way (with mining time), rotates or upgrades what's already there like a click would, and lines coordinates up with the grid.
    - Building ghosts clears trees and rocks in their way.
    - Pole lines hop by wire reach over belts, machines and water.
    - Belt and pipe lines go under crossings and water with undergrounds.
    - Belt lines built in legs chain up: a line from the last belt of another continues it around the corner, a line to an existing belt feeds into it (never head-on), and missing undergrounds or belts get hand-crafted.
    - Walking to a spot nobody can stand on ends at the nearest free spot; a serf caught on a corner wiggles free.
    - diagnose understands fluids (petroleum gas, steam, water, crude oil).
    - Power is visible: inspecting a machine says powered, low power, or no power and the nearest grid pole; inspecting any pole shows its grid (load vs capacity, generators, boiler fuel, top users).
    - Placing, stamping and building warn when machines have no power, and a pole line that doesn't reach the grid says so.
    - craft count is the number of items (copper-cable count=10 is 5 crafts), and a short craft says which ingredient ran out.
    - Inserters say which side they pick from and drop to; underground belts say entrance or exit; look can look around any x,y.
    - trace follows a belt line from any of its belts, downstream or upstream: its runs and underground hops, why it ends (a gap, head-on, side-loading another belt), what's on each lane, and the inserters and drills taking from or putting onto it.
    - Clearer messages when take, insert, mine, place or find can't do what was asked.
    - find with an item name (copper-plate, underground-belt) lists where it's stored: chests and machine outputs, nearest first, with counts.
    - fetch walks to the nearest storage and takes the items it's asked for, then hand-crafts whatever storage lacks.
    - Belt, pipe and pole lines fetch every piece their route needs from storage before hand-crafting it, and say whether each end joined a belt; a start next to a line's last belt continues that line.
